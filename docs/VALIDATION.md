# Gate #5 — Paid Market Pull

ZERO is not considered validated by stars, likes, compliments, benchmark counts, or page views.

## Signal that passes this gate
A real external user/team agrees to pay **$49** for an adversarial scan of an authorized, sanitized agent/MCP workflow.

Useful but weaker signals:
1. a developer submits a real sanitized workflow;
2. a team asks for a pilot;
3. a user reproduces a multi-step violation their normal tests missed;
4. a team asks for repeat/regression scans.

## Live offer
- Free: quick fit assessment only.
- $49: one-off adversarial pilot scan.
- $199/month: future hypothesis only; not sold as a validated plan.

Payment is arranged privately after scope acceptance. No secrets or payment credentials belong in this repository.

## Zero-capital operating constraint
Board capital: **$0**.
Paid acquisition budget: **$0**.
Infrastructure/tooling spend before revenue: **$0**.
Revenue earned by ZERO becomes project treasury for later reinvestment.

## Kill / pivot discipline
If targeted outreach plus real pilot attempts do not produce paid commitment, change the offer/segment or kill the project rather than spending Board money or building a dashboard.
